## Reservations and cancellation

Reservations at Harbor Pasta Lab can be cancelled without a fee at least 24 hours before the booking. Cancellations less than 24 hours before the booking incur a RM20 fee per booking.

## Dietary information

The kitchen handles wheat, milk and eggs. Vegan dishes are prepared in the same kitchen as other dishes. Ask the restaurant about allergies before ordering.
